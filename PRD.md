# SkillSamaritan - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Vision

SkillSamaritan is a platform that connects volunteers with people seeking to learn practical skills.

The platform aims to make education accessible by allowing mentors to donate their knowledge while enabling learners to discover opportunities based on interests, location, and availability.

---

# Problem Statement

Many individuals possess valuable skills but lack a platform to share them.

Similarly, many learners want guidance but cannot afford expensive courses.

SkillSamaritan bridges this gap through community-driven mentorship.

---

- Easy mentor registration
- Skill discovery
- Session scheduling
